Meta Publishing APIs for a Single-Founder Rust Content Studio (as of October 30, 2026)

You can build this without App Review or business verification. Meta’s Instagram Platform Overview says that if the app serves only your own Instagram professional account, “Standard Access is all your app needs”. Advanced Access, which needs App Review and Business Verification, applies only to accounts you don’t own or manage. facebook Use Facebook Login for Business with long-lived Page tokens, keep scheduling in your local app, and first move off Graph API v20.0, which Meta removed on September 24, 2026. facebook

TL;DR


Option A, login path: use Facebook Login for Business with Page tokens (Instagram API with Facebook Login). One login covers your Facebook Pages and your linked Instagram account. Page tokens derived from a long-lived user token “do not have an expiration date”, so you don’t need to log in again on a schedule. Uploading a local video file in resumable chunks (upload_type=resumable) works only on this path. Carousels are fully supported, with images and videos mixed. Meta’s docs disagree on the maximum: the version with v25.0 examples says “Carousels are limited to 10 images, videos, or a mix of the two”, while the version with v26.0 examples says up to 20. Design for 10.
Option B, access level: stay on Standard Access and don’t submit for App Review. Business Verification and the annual Data Use Checkup aren’t required at that level. Meta says data created in Development mode, “such as test posts”, is visible only to role users. So check that your posts are publicly visible before relying on Development mode. If they aren’t, switching to Live mode needs a Data Use Checkup but no App Review.
Option C, scheduling: keep the schedule and the approval queue local. The Instagram Content Publishing API has no scheduling parameter (not found in the docs), and containers expire after 24 hours. Facebook’s native scheduled_publish_time (10 minutes to 30 days ahead) is an optional fallback for Page feed and photo posts when your laptop may be off. Before adding features, migrate to v25.0 or v26.0 and remove the deprecated insights metrics.

What could change this answer


Can you publish to your own accounts without review? Yes. The Instagram Platform Overview says: “If your app only serves your Instagram professional account or an account you manage, Standard Access is all your app needs.” facebook Meta’s App Modes doc says Development-mode apps can request permissions only from role users, at Standard or Advanced access. The catch: the same doc says “Any data generated while an app is in Development mode, such as test posts, can only be seen by role users.” facebook Meta’s “Build and Test” doc adds that app modes “apply to non-business apps”. facebook In a Business-type app the visibility risk may not apply. In a Consumer-type or Development-mode app, check every first post from a logged-out browser.
Is carousel missing from the API? No. media_type=CAROUSEL plus children is documented. facebook
Do Page tokens need you to log in again periodically? No, unless something invalidates them. Meta’s long-lived token guide says Page tokens from long-lived user tokens “do not have an expiration date and only expire or are invalidated under certain conditions”. facebook Common triggers include a password change, losing your Page role, removing the app, or new security requirements on the Page. A GitHub gist (lucnap) says the Page token “has no expiration unless you change your password or not more admin of the target page or deauthorize FB page”. Outstand.so (2026) maps a password change to error 190/460 and a lost Page role to error 492. Instagram Login tokens last 60 days but can be refreshed automatically without a login. facebook

Option B: Development mode vs Standard vs Advanced access


Standard Access is the default for every permission. It works only for people with a role on the app (admin, developer, tester) or on a business portfolio that has claimed the app. No App Review, no Business Verification (Instagram Platform Overview, current). postgatefacebook
Advanced Access is for accounts you don’t own or manage. It “requires App Review and Business Verification” (Overview). facebookfacebook You don’t need it.
Development vs Live mode (App Modes doc): new apps start in Development mode. Live mode needs App Review only for permissions requested from non-role users. In Live mode, Standard-Access permissions can still be requested from role users. facebook The Data Use Checkup doc says you don’t need a checkup in Development mode, but you do “before the app can be switched to Live mode”. Apps with only Standard Access don’t need the annual checkup. facebook
Recommendation: create a Business-type app and stay on Standard Access. Never submit for App Review. If a test post turns out to be visible only to role users, switch to Live mode, which needs a privacy-policy URL and a Data Use Checkup but no App Review.
2FA and Page Publishing Authorization (PPA): publishing fails if the linked Page requires PPA and you haven’t completed it. It also fails if the Page requires two-factor authentication and the Facebook user hasn’t done 2FA (IG User Media reference). facebook Complete PPA upfront.


Option C: Native vs local scheduling


Facebook Page native scheduling is supported. On /feed and /photos, set published=false and scheduled_publish_time (Unix time, ISO 8601, or a strtotime string). The time must be “between 10 minutes and 30 days from the time of the API request” (Pages API Posts guide). facebook

Scheduled multi-photo posts need each photo uploaded with published=false and temporary=true, then a /feed call with attached_media, published=false, scheduled_publish_time and unpublished_content_type=SCHEDULED. Unpublished uploads are deleted after about 24 h if not used (Page Photos reference v26.0). facebook
Page Reels: the video_reels reference lists video_state {DRAFT, PUBLISHED, SCHEDULED} and scheduled_publish_time. facebook A vendor blog (bundle.social) says Reels can’t be scheduled. facebookbundle That’s a conflict, so test it before relying on it.



Instagram native scheduling is not found. The Content Publishing API has no scheduling parameter, and containers expire after 24 h. Meta tells apps to “enforce the publishing rate limit… if your app allows app users to schedule posts”. facebookfacebook In other words, Meta expects the app to do the scheduling.
Recommendation: run all scheduling locally, behind your human-approval gate. That gives one queue, one audit log, and identical behavior on both platforms. Use Facebook native scheduling only for Page feed or photo posts you want published while the app is offline, and record the returned post ID so you can cancel it. The scheduled-post cap is not documented, so don’t design around 350.

Likely causes of the insights HTTP 400


Requesting impressions, plays or video_views on Instagram media or user insights (removed April 21, 2025 and January 8, 2025). facebook +2
Requesting Page *_impressions_unique or reach metrics after June 2026. facebook
Combining a metric with an unsupported breakdown or metric_type. The IG User Insights doc warns that this returns an error. facebook
Asking for user insights on an account with fewer than 100 followers, or for media with too few viewers (“(#10) Not enough viewers”). facebookfacebook
Requesting Story metrics after the 24 h Story window, or using the wrong period.


Log the error.code, error_subcode and message on every 400.

Recommended stack (my recommendation; crate versions not verified here):

reqwest (rustls, multipart, streaming body) with serde/serde_json typed request and response structs.
A typed GraphError { code, error_subcode, message, fbtrace_id }.
The oauth2 crate with a loopback redirect for the occasional Facebook login.
OS keychain storage for tokens (e.g. the keyring crate).
tokio for container-status polling.



Polling: Meta recommends querying container status “once per minute, for no more than 5 minutes”. The states are EXPIRED, ERROR, FINISHED, IN_PROGRESS and PUBLISHED. facebook
Version: pin a single GRAPH_VERSION const (v25.0 now; v26.0 once you have tested it). Read X-Business-Use-Case-Usage and x-app-usage headers into your log.
Idempotency: a publish that times out may already be live. After any timeout, check GET /{container}?fields=status_code before retrying, so you never post twice.
