DECISION: For one founder publishing only to their own X, Threads and Bluesky accounts from a local Rust app, which official API path to build on for each, and what is not worth building.
OPTIONS: X: free tier, pay-per-use credits, fixed paid tier, or no API (paste by hand). Threads: development-mode app only, or full Meta app review. Bluesky: app password or atproto OAuth; Rust SDK or raw XRPC.
CONSTRAINTS: Own accounts only; Rust; local-first; every post approved by the human first. As of October 2026, dated primary sources (developer docs, changelogs, pricing pages); say "not found".
NEED, per platform: what an approved single-user app may do through the official API: publish text, 1-4 images with alt text, multi-image or carousel, video, reply chains, schedule, delete; read own post metrics and replies; reply; read public posts, search, trends. Auth model and token lifetimes (refresh, rotation); app review and its duration; rate limits and price per post and per read; terms limits on automation, AI-generated content and data use; 2025-2026 breaking changes; sandbox or test mode; maintained Rust crates with last release date.
CURRENT EVIDENCE TO CONFIRM OR CORRECT (March 2026, unverified): X Free 500 writes a month, no reads; Basic $200; pay-per-use since February 2026 (about $0.01 per post, $0.005 per read); OAuth 2.0 PKCE scopes tweet.write, media.write, offline.access; an older client uploaded images through the v1.1 media endpoint. Threads: 500 characters, 250 posts a day, 60-day refreshable tokens, carousels up to 20 items, 10,000-character text attachment. Bluesky: 300 graphemes, session-creation limits, no official analytics.
WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER: X posting for about 30 posts a month costing more than a few dollars, or media upload needing a paid tier; Threads publishing blocked until a review a solo app cannot pass.
PUBLIC CONTEXT: A pre-launch single-user content studio drafts posts and publishes only after explicit approval.
