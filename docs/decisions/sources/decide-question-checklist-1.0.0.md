# Question checklist

Version: 1.0.0

Use this checklist before requesting decision research. It checks question preparation,
not whether the answer is true, complete or best. Stable item IDs survive wording changes.

For each item, record pass, repair or unknown with a reason and an evidence locator.
A filled field proves only presence. Read the evidence before judging its substance.
Keep missing facts and disagreements visible; never convert unknown into a favorable score.

Freeze decision criteria, pass marks and case/source identities before outside evidence arrives.
Preserve that version. Record later amendments separately, with their reason and timing.

## Q1 Decision

Pass: State the choice to make, desired outcome, decision owner and reversibility.
Separate the choice from a request to justify a preferred solution.

Repair: Rewrite the question around the choice and outcome. Name unresolved owner decisions;
do not invent approval or treat research advice as permission to act.

## Q2 Options

Pass: Name real alternatives, including doing nothing or keeping the current approach.
Give each option's different mechanism and the reason it remains feasible.
Record why a seemingly relevant option is excluded.

Repair: Add the missing baseline or materially different option. Remove cosmetic variants
that conceal the same approach. Do not claim the list exhausts every possible future.

## Q3 Constraints

Pass: Distinguish hard constraints from preferences and assumptions.
Give each consequential constraint its source, scope and applicable time.
Expose conflicts and who can resolve them.

Repair: Find the governing source or mark the constraint unverified.
Ask the owner about an unresolved hard boundary; do not quietly relax it to favor an option.

## Q4 Current numbers

Pass: Provide decision-relevant baseline measurements with units, dates, scope and sources.
Separate measured values, reported values and estimates.
For unavailable numbers, state what is missing, why it matters and how to obtain it.

Repair: Replace unsupported precision with an explicit unknown.
Obtain the relevant measurement or narrow the question to what the evidence can answer.
A reported number with a citation is not independently verified.

## Q5 What changes the answer

Pass: Name decision-changing unknowns and the evidence or threshold that would favor another option.
Tie them to the frozen criteria. Distinguish researchable facts from locally measurable uncertainties.

Repair: Replace "research everything" with the unknown that could reverse the choice.
When measurement can settle it, propose a bounded experiment with a predeclared pass mark
instead of more research. Do not invent a numeric threshold the owner has not set.

## Q6 Independent challenger

Pass: Prepare a secondary question that tests another option, a decisive assumption or a failure mechanism.
Ask where the primary approach fails, which techniques lack transferable evidence,
and what simpler procedure could do as well. Supply the same relevant facts and constraints.
For a consequential or hard-to-reverse choice, plan one challenger from a different model family.

Repair: Replace a paraphrase or request for agreement with a concrete competing explanation or option.
Record the planned family and evidence boundary, not just an "independent" label.
If unavailable, disclose the gap. A fresh same-family review is not cross-family challenge;
neither a different family nor a second call proves independence or correctness.

## Handoff

Retain the question, context, item dispositions and frozen criteria with the study record.
Unresolved repairs are visible gaps, not a quality certificate or permission to Send.
This checklist neither submits research nor chooses providers, order or budgets.

Consumers copy these exact bytes and record version, SHA256 and source commit beside the copy.
A changed copy needs a new reviewed pin; a live symlink is not a pinned copy.
